把边界说清楚。

Clear boundaries.

这是已经验证的AI自动化产品吗？Is this a validated AI automation product?

不是。现有合成测试使用确定性规则与固定模板，没有运行时大模型，也没有真实客户效果证据。

No. Existing synthetic tests use deterministic rules and fixed templates, without a runtime language model or validated customer outcomes.

可以直接发三段邮件摘要吗？Can I send three email summaries now?

不可以。当前不接收真实客户材料，候选程序会暂停非空自由文本，不具备原始邮件解析或一般语义理解能力。

Not at this stage. We are not accepting real customer material. The candidate holds non-empty free text and does not parse raw emails or provide general semantic understanding.

是否需要现场演示或讲课？Is a live demonstration or training required?

不需要。标准沟通采用书面说明和可检查的合成结果，不提供专家讲课或现场技术诊断。

No. Standard communication uses written explanations and inspectable synthetic output, not expert-led training or live technical diagnosis.

是否承诺价格、交期或成交效果？Are price, turnaround or sales outcomes promised?

目前不承诺。这是需求验证入口，不是在线购买页面；没有付款、上传或自动发信功能。

No. This is a discovery page, not an online purchasing service. There is no payment, upload or automatic-email feature.
