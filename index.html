FAQ

先回答最容易卡住的四件事。

Four questions that usually block a first test.

必须整理完整邮件吗？Do I need to prepare full email threads?

不需要。首测只要三段脱敏摘要，写明产品、国家、关键参数、是否报价和最后一次跟进结果即可。

No. For the first test, three anonymized summaries with product, country, key parameters, quotation status and last follow-up outcome are enough.

会自动联系我的客户吗？Will InquiryFlow contact my buyers automatically?

不会。免费诊断和 Sprint 默认只输出草稿与队列，发送权仍由企业授权人员掌握。

No. The diagnostic and Sprint output drafts and queues only. Sending authority stays with your team.

可以处理中文、英文混合内容吗？Can it handle mixed Chinese and English records?

可以，但样本中仍应删除个人信息，并说明原始语言和产品类别。

Yes, provided personal identifiers are removed and the original language and product category are stated.

为什么不承诺回复率？Why is no response-rate guarantee offered?

回复还受到产品、价格、市场时机、原始客户质量和发件域名信誉影响。我们只承诺可验收的分析与交付质量，不把不可控变量包装成保证。

Replies depend on product, price, timing, original lead quality and sender reputation. We commit to inspectable analysis and deliverables, not uncontrollable outcomes.
